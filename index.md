# Collegeville 2022

**This workshop was not held.** Due to continued complications from the pandemic, St. John's University Events was challenged to provide adequate staffing for on-site events. Rather than conduct Collegeville 2022 as a purely virtual event, the organizing committee postponed the meeting to the week of July 24, 2023. The 2023 workshop was later canceled because of continued logistics and staffing challenges.

See the [Collegeville Workshops page](https://collegeville.github.io/workshops/) for the full series.
